# E3 link-failure experiment (B4, GEANT, UsCarrier)

Failure cases: `data/scenarios/link_failures` (P1 §5.5 sampler; seeds 100–1000; 0–3 failed directed edges). Per case, paths through failed links are dropped and Yen k=4 is recomputed for pairs left without a path; demand of pairs disconnected by the failure is unservable and counted as undelivered.

TMs: B4 val (8), UsCarrier val (1; identical toy copies), GEANT 50 seeded test TMs. Load: fixed {0.5, 1.0} and calibrated {0.7, 0.9} (alpha from the intact topology). All LPs use SciPy HiGHS (scipy.optimize.linprog, method='highs'). No Gurobi licence was available, so Teal/FIGRET-style Gurobi LPs are replaced by HiGHS. LP here is the min-MLU LP over the surviving k=4 candidates.

Framework rows (`fw_<policy>_<variant>`): A no gate, D mean-only gate, E calibrated-uncertainty gate (k=1, frozen), F physics oracle; rejected proposals fall back to shortest/equal (least-violating).

The P1 §5.5-comparable table for B4 (frozen P1 with its own ADMM pipeline) is in `baseline_p1_reproduction/phase3_final/results/b4_link_failures/`.
